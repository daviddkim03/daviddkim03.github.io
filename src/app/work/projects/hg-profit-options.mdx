## Overview

Trading education depends on timely information. HG Profit Options gives members one place to get alerts, study the method, and track their own trades, with a public site that explains the program.

## Technical Highlights

- **Real-time alerts**: delivered in the app, in the browser, and by email.
- **Member dashboard**: weekly strike-price targets and a channel ladder chart.
- **Trading journal**: manual entry and CSV import, with win rate and streaks.
- **Classes and community**: live schedule, classroom, and a files library.

## Technologies Used

- **Next.js + TypeScript**: public site and member area
- **Firebase**: authentication, data, and hosting
