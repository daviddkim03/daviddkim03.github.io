## Overview

ProgressReport turns Prime Academy's ClassMarker results into SAT progress reports. Staff can view a student's report, download it as a PDF, or email it to the student and their parents.

## Technical Highlights

- **Webhook-first sync**: new results arrive within seconds without spending ClassMarker's limit of 30 API requests per hour.
- **Cached reads**: pages are served from the server's cache and never wait for ClassMarker.
- **Scoring sheets**: an importer reads Excel, CSV, or pasted tables and converts raw scores to scaled section scores.
- **Programs and exams**: each exam maps its four sections to tests and the scoring sheet it uses.
- **Bulk email**: reports go to many students at once, now or on a schedule.

## Technologies Used

- **React + Vite, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui**: staff interface
- **Node.js + Express**: API and ClassMarker sync
- **SQLite**: results store
- **Puppeteer**: PDF rendering
