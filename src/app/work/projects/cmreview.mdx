## Overview

cmReview helps instructors decide what to go over after a test. It drills down by group, subtest, and question, and ranks every question by how often students missed it.

## Technical Highlights

- **Question ranking**: incorrect and unanswered responses count as missed, and questions needing manual grading are excluded.
- **Full detail**: question text, options, correct answer, answer distribution, and individual attempts.
- **Incremental sync**: pulls only the results recorded since the last sync from a token-protected export.
- **Local cache**: attempts and deduplicated question content are stored by the backend, with no direct ClassMarker access needed.

## Technologies Used

- **React + Vite**: review interface
- **Node.js + Express**: sync, aggregation, and API
