## Overview

Sky Arc Sports Academy trains young air rifle athletes. Its platform combines a public website with the tools its coaches, office, and families use.

## Technical Highlights

- **Scheduling**: classes and coach availability in one place.
- **Time and payroll**: kiosk clock-in, time entries, and payroll.
- **Target files**: scoring PDFs are read and filed for each student on upload.
- **Progress reports**: families can follow results over time.
- **Phone app**: built for families, coaches, and the office.

## Technologies Used

- **TypeScript + Vite**: website and portals
- **Firebase**: authentication, Firestore, storage, Cloud Functions, and hosting
